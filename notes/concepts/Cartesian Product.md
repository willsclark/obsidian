# Cartesian Product

requires:: 
source:: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_product 


## Intuition

Take a matrix of rows / columns. The cartesian product is the set of all row / column pairs. 

## Definition 

*Defn* The _Cartesian Product_ of a set $A$ and $B$, denoted $A \times B$ is the set of all ordered pairs, $(a, b)$, where $a \in A, \; b \in B$. 
$$
A \times B = \left\{ (a, b) : a \in A, b \in B \right\} 
$$

*Defn* The _n-ary Cartesian Product_ over $n$ sets ${}A_{1}, A_{2}, \dots A_{n}{}$ is the set of $n$-tuples 
$$
A_{1} \times A_{2} \times \dots \times A_{n} = \left\{ (a_{1}, a_{2}, \dots, a_{n}) : a_{i} \in A_{i} \right\} 
$$
